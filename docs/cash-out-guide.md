# Cash-out guide (first version)

Work Deposits never holds your money. Payments go straight into your own Lightning wallet. To
turn sats into local money, use a partner service. We only link to them. We do not connect to
them, send them data, or take a fee.

## Partners

| Partner | What it says it does | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Mavapay | Receives bitcoin over Lightning and pays out local money to a bank account. Third-party sources list Nigeria, Kenya and South Africa. | https://app.mavapay.money/ |
| Bitnob | Wallet and payouts for people and businesses. Its home page does not state a bitcoin cash-out or a country list. | https://bitnob.com/ |

## Check before you rely on one

- Open the partner's own site and confirm it serves your country today.
- Try a very small amount first.
- Fees and rates change. Compare the amount you receive with what you expected.
