Project and Submission Requirements
The project should at least have:

A presentation (text or video)
A public github repo
A MVP accessible through internet (or a demo video)
Prizes
1st prize: 500.000 sats
2nd prize: 250.000 sats
3rd prize: 100.000 sats


Judging Criteria and Winner Selection
The judges will mainly focused on solutions that offers a MVP that can be tested and which gives a glimpse on its potential. Besides that, we will also judge the originality, the usefulness of the solution and the technicality.

We are looking for:

Usefulness and social impact
Security by design
UX/UI quality
MVP
A clear pitch

Requirements

What to Build
We are expecting Bitcoin, nostr and AI-centered solutions which main aim would be to answer local and regional population needs. Those solutions can be (these are just examples):

Wallets
Learning tools
Exchanges
A contribution to Bitcoin core or lightning
A messaging app built on top of Nostr
An authentication method using NWC
Bitcoin & AI

What to Submit
A brief presentation of the proposed solution (can be text or video)
A prototype, Minimum Viable Product (MVP) or Proof of Concept (Poc)
At least, a bitcoin/lightning wallet implementation
